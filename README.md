# SwiftShip Tracker

**Smart Shipment Tracking and Management System**

## Project Overview

SwiftShip Tracker is a Salesforce-based shipment management system designed to simplify and streamline the process of tracking shipments, managing customers, and monitoring delivery operations from a centralized platform.

## Objectives

* Track shipments and delivery status efficiently
* Manage customer and shipment information
* Monitor delivery progress
* Reduce manual tracking processes
* Improve shipment visibility and operational efficiency
* Provide centralized access to logistics data

## Salesforce Features Used

* Custom Objects and Fields
* Salesforce Lightning
* Salesforce Flow
* Automation
* Reports and Dashboards
* Data Management
* Security and Access Controls

## Key Features

* Shipment Management
* Customer Management
* Tracking and Delivery Status
* Automated Shipment Updates
* Delivery Monitoring

## Technology

* **Platform:** Salesforce CRM
* **Interface:** Salesforce Lightning
* **Automation:** Salesforce Flow
* **Reporting:** Salesforce Reports and Dashboards

## Project Outcome

SwiftShip Tracker demonstrates how Salesforce can be used to build an efficient shipment tracking and logistics management solution. The system improves shipment visibility, reduces manual work, and helps organizations manage delivery operations effectively.
